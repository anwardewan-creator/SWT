About this task type

In Summarize Written Text, you read a passage of up to 300 words and write a single-sentence summary of it, between 5 and 75 words, in 10 minutes. This task draws on both your reading and writing skills.

Scoring — the official PTE method

  • Content (0–4): how well your sentence captures the main points of the passage.
  • Form (0–1): a single sentence, 5–75 words, starting with a capital letter and ending with a full stop. A response that isn't one sentence, or is written all in capitals, is not scored at all.
  • Grammar (0–2): correct grammatical structure throughout.
  • Vocabulary (0–2): appropriate word choice, ideally paraphrasing the passage rather than copying it directly.
  • Maximum score per item: 9.
This workbook can reliably auto-check Form (word count, single sentence, capitalization, punctuation) and can approximate Content by checking whether your sentence covers the passage's key points. Grammar and Vocabulary need genuine language judgement that a static page can't perform, so instead of guessing a number, this workbook shows you the official band descriptors and lets you self-assess honestly against them — same as the real scoring criteria, just scored by you instead of an examiner.

Strategy: before writing

  • Take your time to read the passage calmly. Skim first for the general topic, then read carefully for the main ideas.
  • Note the main idea and supporting ideas using keywords, symbols, or arrows to show how the ideas connect.
  • Effective note-taking ensures your summary contains all the main points — but don't spend too long on it; a few minutes is enough.

Strategy: while writing

  • Type the main point first, then add supporting details.
  • Your summary must be a single sentence of 5–75 words: start with a capital letter, end with a full stop.
  • Watch your grammar and punctuation as you write.
  • Use Cut, Copy and Paste (your keyboard's normal Ctrl/Cmd+X, C, V) to rearrange text if needed.
  • Keep an eye on the time and your word count as you go.

Strategy: after writing

  • Check whether your sentence covers the theme of the whole passage.
  • Recheck your grammar and vocabulary.
  • Confirm your sentence starts with a capital letter, ends with a full stop, and is within the 5–75 word limit.
  • If you finish early, review your answer before time runs out — once the 10 minutes end, you can no longer edit your response.
